Hello, I’m Kalyani.

I’ve always loved travelling, especially to places that carry stories of the past.

Old forts, beautiful temples, painted walls, traditional homes, local markets and handmade crafts have a special place in my heart. I often find myself spending more time admiring a carved doorway or an old painting than taking in the usual tourist attractions.

Beyond the usual tourist spots

Sometimes, the most memorable part of a journey isn’t the famous monument.

It might be a centuries-old painting on a haveli wall, a conversation with a local artist, a beautifully carved wooden door, or a quiet moment beside a lake.

These are the details I love discovering. And these are the stories I want to share.

More miles. More memories.

There are still so many places I want to explore, stories I want to hear, and traditions I want to understand. This journal will grow with every journey.

Thank you for being part of it.

— Kalyani
