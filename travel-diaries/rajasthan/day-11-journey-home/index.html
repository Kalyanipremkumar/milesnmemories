    Day 11 — The Journey Home

    Day 11, Tuesday 20 October

    Udaipur to Bengaluru: the journey home

    A morning at the airport and a suitcase full of memories.

    Our last morning began with the practical business of leaving: packing, checking out and reaching the airport. We were waiting at the gate for our 9:50 a.m. flight from Udaipur to Bengaluru. After ten busy days of sightseeing and eleven calendar days away, it was finally time to go home.

    It is difficult to choose a single image that represents Rajasthan for me. It might be Jaipur’s terracotta streets, a carved wooden door in Mandawa, the stepwell that brought back Sirivennela, a richly painted room at Junagarh, the sunset at Bada Bagh, or the moon above the Khuri dunes. It might also be a smaller moment: sitar music at breakfast, coffee before an unexpected meeting, lunch at my friend’s home, or a conversation with an artist about pigments and brushes.

    I returned with purchases and photographs, of course. But the more lasting souvenirs were ideas: patterns to study, details to remember, stories to retell, and a renewed appreciation for the people who keep traditional crafts alive. I had wanted to visit Rajasthan for more than a decade. It was worth the wait.
