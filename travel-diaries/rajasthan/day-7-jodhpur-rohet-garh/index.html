Day 7, Friday 16 October

Jodhpur: the Blue City and royal comfort

Forts, marble, markets and a stay that felt truly special.

After the desert, we continued to Jodhpur, another city with a distinct identity. Jaipur is associated with pink, Jaisalmer with golden sandstone, and Jodhpur with the blue-painted houses visible in parts of its older neighbourhoods. The colours are useful labels, but each city is much richer than its nickname.

The fort above the city

Mehrangarh Fort dominates the skyline. Its towering walls and elevated position create a sense of scale that is difficult to capture in a photograph. Inside, the palace areas and museum collections reveal the ceremonial and artistic world of Marwar’s rulers. The views over Jodhpur are impressive, but the interiors deserve just as much attention. For me, Rajasthan’s forts were never only about defence; they were also repositories of craft and memory.

We also visited Jaswant Thada, the elegant marble memorial associated with Maharaja Jaswant Singh II. Its pale stone and quieter atmosphere contrast with Mehrangarh’s massive walls. Umaid Bhawan Palace brought us into a different era of royal architecture, with its twentieth-century design and distinctive scale. We covered the Clock Tower area and Sardar Market as well, seeing the busy commercial life of the old city alongside its grand monuments.

A hotel worth remembering

Our stay at Rohet Garh, in Rohet village, was among the best of the entire journey. It was clean, neat and beautifully maintained, and I loved the sense of Rajasthani royalty in the surroundings. If I were giving it a personal rating, it would be five stars. After a succession of very different hotels, this one stood out for its quality and atmosphere.

Jodhpur also reinforced something I had been noticing throughout Rajasthan: the extraordinary effort that has gone into preserving royal buildings, traditional architecture and cultural forms. Not everything about travel was perfect, but the heritage itself continued to impress me. All the places we visited in Jodhpur were enjoyable; the day was simply a very good one.
